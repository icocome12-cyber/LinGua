## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a welcoming, joyful, and multi-generational educational environment designed specifically for dual English and Twi step-by-step language acquisition. Balancing kid-safe approachability with adult dignity, the design language rejects infantilizing clichés while retaining tangible, playful affordances. 

The aesthetic is characterized by a "Tactile Modernist Sprout" philosophy. It blends crisp, contemporary digital execution with tactile, physical push-buttons, rhythmic typography, and organic botanical progression motifs (sprouts, seedling pots, and growth rings). The tone is encouraging, bright, culturally honoring, and structured. 

To maintain distinct identity and avoid common genre tropes:
- Strictly prohibit emerald/lime green gamification tropes and avian or owl mascots.
- Rely on rich indigo-violet, radiant warm coral, sunlit yellow, and fresh aquatic teal.
- Express visual delight through physically tactile, dimensional UI interactions (extruded pressable depths) paired with spacious, calming typography.

## Colors

The palette balances energy, cultural warmth, and pedagogical focus. All contrast ratios meet or exceed WCAG AA standards against their respective surfaces.

### Functional Palette Mapping
- **Primary (`#6C4DF6`) & Primary Dark (`#5436D6`):** The structural core. Represents confidence and discovery. `#5436D6` is utilized for tactile button extrusions, active states, and grounding borders.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A59`):** Radiant coral used for high-urgency call-to-actions, phonetic milestones, and energetic praise moments.
- **Tertiary (`#FFC845`):** Warm sunlit yellow for celebration chips, streak rewards, and pronunciation star-ratings.
- **Success (`#2EC4B6`):** Balanced aquatic teal signaling correct answers, lesson completion, and flourishing growth vectors.
- **Danger (`#EF476F`):** Gentle rose signaling missteps or required reviews without punitive harshness.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Canvas Base: `#FDFCFB` (warm off-white foundation that softens eye strain).
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF` (elevated cards and modular panels).
  - Surface Muted: `#F4F1FF` (subtle lavender tint used for inset lesson tiles and phonetic breakdowns).
- **Ink System:**
  - Ink Deep (`#1F1B2E`): High-clarity heading and essential label text.
  - Ink Muted (`#6B6680`): Explanatory body text and cross-translations.
  - Ink Soft (`#9D98B0`): Borders, placeholder copy, and inactive trackers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally pairs the open, humanist geometry of **Nunito Sans** for display, headlines, and interactive action labels with the neutral, hyper-legible clarity of **Inter** for bilingual instructional body copy.

- **Nunito Sans (Display & Headlines):** Rendered in weights 700 to 900 to give the Twi and English headings rhythmic, inviting personality without looking informal or childish. Rounded stroke terminals reinforce the sprout shape motif.
- **Inter (Body & Pedagogy):** Delivers clean readability for pronunciation guides, orthographic tone markers in Twi (e.g., ɛ, ɔ), and dual-language comparative tables.
- **Phonetic & Tonal Rendering:** When displaying Twi characters, letter spacing must remain neutral (`0em`) to ensure tone diacritics sit cleanly over target vowels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Structural Parameters
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max layout container width of `1200px` centered with variable outer margins (minimum `2rem`). Gutters are locked at `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins. Modules reflow from 3 across to 2 across.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Dual-language comparison modules stack vertically into step cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical spacing between landing page sections leverages `space-xl` multiplied by two (`5rem`) to create open, calm transitions. Micro-rhythms inside cards follow strict 8px increments using `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), and `space-lg` (24px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile, dimensional physical planes rather than diffuse, hazy drop shadows. Surfaces mimic premium matte educational materials that feel physical and pressable.

### 1. Tactile 3D Extrusion (Buttons & Interactive Cards)
- Primary interactives use a solid base offset: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #5436D6`.
- Active / pressed state shifts the element downward by `2px` to `4px` while reducing the shadow to `0 0 0 0`, simulating a mechanical, tactile switch.
- Secondary interactives (Coral) apply the same mechanism using an offset tone of `#DE5836`.

### 2. Ambient Layering (Cards & Surface Modulars)
- Cards float above the `#FDFCFB` canvas with a soft ambient perimeter: `box-shadow: 0 6px 20px -4px rgba(31, 27, 46, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(31, 27, 46, 0.04)`.
- Cards maintain a crisp hairline border (`1.5px solid rgba(108, 77, 246, 0.08)`) to preserve clean separation without heavy visual noise.

### 3. Inset Surfaces
- Lesson previews, audio-syllable tiles, and script sandbox zones utilize an inset background (`#F4F1FF`) with zero elevation, sitting flush into the white parent card.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses an organic, welcoming geometry anchored by continuous curves and smooth silhouettes.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized to a generous `20px` border radius (`rounded-lg` level) creating soft, friendly visual containers.
- **Buttons & Large Interactive Elements:** Fixed at `16px` corner radius. This balances the 3D extrusion beneath the button without distorting corner geometry.
- **Pills, Badges & Tags:** Locked at `999px` full-stadium radius. Used exclusively for progress indicators, level labels (e.g., "Beginner • A1"), and vocabulary chips.
- **Sprout Graphic Motifs:** Vector accents, speech tail pointers, and progress bars adopt parabolic, organic curves matching the curvature of a budding leaf.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Extruded):** Background `#6C4DF6`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `16px`, height `56px`, font `label-lg`. Features a bottom shadow of `0 4px 0 0 #5436D6`. On `:hover`, subtle brightness increase. On `:active`, transforms `translateY(4px)` with shadow reduced to zero.
- **Secondary Action (Warm Coral):** Background `#FF7A59`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `16px`, height `56px`. Bottom shadow of `0 4px 0 0 #DE5836`. Same physical compression on click.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Background `transparent`, border `2px solid #6C4DF6`, text `#6C4DF6`, radius `16px`. Hover background `#F4F1FF`.

### 2. Language & Phonetic Chips
- **Syllable Audio Chips:** Stadium pill shape (`999px`), padding `8px 16px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #F4F1FF`, shadow `0 2px 0 0 #E2DCFF`. Left-aligned speaker icon followed by dual orthography (e.g., `Akwaaba` / `Welcome`).

### 3. Cards & Lesson Modules
- Base card: Pure white `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `20px`, padding `24px`.
- Step Progression Cards: Feature a step-counter pill at the top, a seedling vector icon that progressively unfurls leaves across steps 1 through 4, dual-language typography block, and an embedded audio sample box styled in `#F4F1FF`.

### 4. Input Fields
- Container: Height `56px`, radius `16px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #E2DCFF`, text `#1F1B2E`. Focus state transitions border to `#6C4DF6` with a soft outer ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(108, 77, 246, 0.15)`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio Controls: Size `24px`, circle, border `2.5px solid #9D98B0`. Selected state features `#6C4DF6` border with an inner `#6C4DF6` solid disc and an animated spring scale.
- Checkboxes: Size `24px`, radius `8px`, border `2px solid #9D98B0`. Checked state renders filled `#2EC4B6` (Teal) with a bold white checkmark.

### 6. Specialized Bilingual Translation Bar
- A dual-panel comparative component with a left English chamber and right Twi chamber separated by an exchange sprout glyph. Both chambers use `#F4F1FF` backgrounds with instant audio playback triggers.
